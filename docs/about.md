# About Policy Hub

Policy Hub is a centralized, Markdown-powered home for the privacy policies,
terms, support pages, data-deletion instructions, and developer links for a
collection of Android applications.

## Why this site exists

Legal and support information should be easy to find, read, and keep current.
Each app has a portal area for browsing related resources and a permanent,
standalone privacy-policy page suitable for a Google Play listing.

## Publishing principles

- Policies must describe the released app accurately.
- Sample text must be reviewed and replaced before publication.
- App permissions, third-party SDKs, and data practices must be disclosed.
- Public policy pages do not require an account, PIN, or JavaScript to read.
- No analytics, tracking, authentication, backend, or database is enabled.
