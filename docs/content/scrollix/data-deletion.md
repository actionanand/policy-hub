# Delete Scrollix Data

Scrollix has no general-purpose developer-operated user account.

## Delete local data

Remove offline posts individually from the Posts screen. Logging out removes the local username and login hash. To remove all feed and preview caches, offline HTML, preferences, PIN, and biometric settings, clear Scrollix storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. In a browser, clear the site's localStorage, session storage, cache, and site data.

## Delete external data

Clearing Scrollix does not delete configured Google Sheet rows, third-party platform records, browser or platform history, clipboard history, or links copied elsewhere. Delete each through the relevant account, Sheet owner, platform, browser, or operating-system controls.

The developer cannot remotely delete data held only on your device or by a third party. For help, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com** without attaching private or restricted content.
