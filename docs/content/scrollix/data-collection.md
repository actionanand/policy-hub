# Scrollix Data Collection

**Last reviewed:** August 1, 2026

## Feed and local data

Scrollix reads configured Google Sheet rows containing media types, URLs or IDs, protection flags, titles, descriptions, start times, and hashes. It caches the feed in unencrypted localStorage. Link-preview metadata and up to five full offline post pages may also be cached locally.

The app stores opening preferences, username and successful password hash, and optional PIN-verifier and biometric settings. Android performs biometric verification without exposing biometric templates to Scrollix.

## External processing

Google, embedded media platforms, destination websites, and Microlink where used receive content requests, URLs or identifiers, and standard connection metadata. Scrollix has no developer-operated advertising, analytics, or telemetry.
