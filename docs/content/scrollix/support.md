# Scrollix Support

For support, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com**.

Include the app version, Android or browser version, device model, content type, and safe reproduction steps. Do not send passwords, PINs, private links, copyrighted downloads, or complete cached pages.

## Common help

- **Feed does not load:** check internet access and confirm the configured Google Sheet remains available.
- **A video will not play:** the platform may block embedding, require login, restrict your region, or remove the content. Open the original page.
- **A preview is wrong or missing:** reload it or open the source. Publishers control Open Graph metadata.
- **Protected content is hidden:** use local login. The Protected flag does not change source permissions.
- **Offline post will not save:** Android supports up to five posts; remove an existing saved post and retry.
- **Forgotten login or PIN:** credentials cannot be recovered by the developer. Clearing app storage also removes offline content and preferences.
