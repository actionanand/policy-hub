# Delete Who Called Data

Who Called has no online account or developer-operated cloud database. Its app
records are stored encrypted on your device.

## Delete contacts

Move contacts to **Trash**. Restore them within 30 days or permanently delete
selected contacts from Trash. You can also empty Trash. Contacts still in Trash
are automatically removed after 30 days when the app loads.

## Delete other records

Delete saved messages and tagged numbers from their corresponding screens.

## Delete all local data

Clear Who Called storage in Android system settings or uninstall the app. In a
browser, clear the site's IndexedDB/site data.

## Delete files outside the app

Clearing app data does not remove `.contactvault` backups, CSV exports, or vCard
files saved or shared outside Who Called. Delete those copies separately from
every destination.

The optional call-history list is not stored by Who Called. Android manages the
device call log separately. Because the developer holds no account data,
emailing support cannot delete records stored on your device.
