# Delete Office Orbit Data

## Delete local session and security data

Use **Settings → Sign out** to remove the stored Office Orbit session and clear the app's in-memory work-data cache.

If PIN protection is enabled, use the Security section in Settings to turn off PIN protection. This removes the local PIN verifier and biometric-unlock preference.

To remove all app-held data on Android, clear Office Orbit storage in Android system settings or uninstall the app. In a browser, close the browser session and clear the Office Orbit site's storage and cache.

## Delete exported reports

PDF reports saved or shared outside Office Orbit are controlled by their destination. Delete every copy from device storage, cloud storage, downloads, email, messaging, or another app where you placed it.

## Delete source work records

Clearing Office Orbit does not delete work logs, JIRAs, sprints, releases, feedback, work links, or other records held by the API or its underlying data source. Request deletion from the administrator or owner of that work-data system, subject to workplace authorization and retention requirements.

## Delete support correspondence

To request deletion of information you sent in a support email, contact **anand.official.in@gmail.com** from the same email address and identify the correspondence. Some records may be retained when required for security, legal, or legitimate support purposes.
