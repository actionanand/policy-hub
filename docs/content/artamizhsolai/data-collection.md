# AR Tamizh Solai Data Collection

**Last reviewed:** August 1, 2026

## Information You Submit

The contact form sends your name, email, inquiry purpose, message, and any optional phone number or content URL to Google Forms. Google and the publisher process and retain the submission.

## Information Stored in Your Browser

For a password-protected article, the site can store a successful password hash and timestamp in localStorage for up to 24 hours. It can temporarily store a pending protected route in sessionStorage. The plain-text password is not stored or sent to an AR Tamizh Solai server. Site search runs locally against published content.

## Technical and Third-Party Information

Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages can receive ordinary hosting logs. Media embeds receive connection and interaction data when loaded or used. Social, citation, and donation services receive requests when you open them, and a selected payment provider processes the information required for a donation.

No reader accounts, public comments, newsletter, or visitor analytics service were found in the reviewed production source.
