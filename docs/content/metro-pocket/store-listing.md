MetroPocket helps you explore metro routes and look up TV channels using downloadable data packs.

Plan a metro journey

• Find stations and routes in supported metro networks
• View journey estimates and route details from installed data
• Use downloaded network data without a connection

Look up TV channels

• Search and filter channel listings where a TV pack is available
• Browse provider information stored in the installed pack
• Refer to channel numbers offline

Manage offline data

• See available packs in the Offline Data screen
• Download the packs you want and check for updates
• Remove a pack when you no longer need it

MetroPocket is available on Android and the web. Its offline features require the relevant packs to be installed first. Internet access is needed for the initial web load and optional pack downloads or updates.

Routes, estimates, and channel listings are informational and may be incomplete or outdated. They are not live train or TV service data. Confirm important details with the relevant operator or provider.

No account, ads, developer-operated analytics, or tracking SDK is required.
