# MetroPocket Support

For help with MetroPocket, email [anand.official.in@gmail.com](mailto:anand.official.in@gmail.com). Include the app version, Android or browser version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include sensitive information unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.

## Common help

- **No metro or TV results:** Open **Offline Data**, check the available packs, and install the pack for the network or provider you need. TV listings depend on the installed data and may not be available for every provider.
- **Pack will not download:** Check your internet connection and available device/browser storage, then retry from **Offline Data**. Pack downloads are optional; installed packs can be used offline.
- **Web app appears empty after reinstalling or clearing browser data:** Browser site data can be removed or evicted. Reopen the app online and install the needed packs again.
- **Route or channel information looks wrong:** The app uses published pack snapshots, not live operator data. Check for a pack update and verify important details with the operator or provider.
