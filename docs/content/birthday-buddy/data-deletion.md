# Birthday Buddy Data Deletion

**Last Updated:** August 26, 2026

Birthday Buddy has no developer-operated account or cloud database. Your app data is controlled on the device or browser where you use the app.

## Delete selected information

Use Birthday Buddy's person and occasion controls to remove records you no longer want. Removing a record deletes its locally stored details and app-owned photo copy. It does not delete or edit the corresponding entry in Android Contacts.

Contact-sync exclusions remain local. Use the contact-sync controls to allow an ignored contact again, or clear all app data as described below.

## Delete all Android app data

Use Android's system settings for Birthday Buddy and choose **Storage & cache > Clear storage** (wording varies by device), or uninstall the app. This removes the app's local database, preferences, contact links, photos, reminder schedules, and lock configuration from that installation.

## Delete browser app data

Use your browser's site-data settings to clear stored data for `actionanand.github.io`. Browser controls and wording vary. Clearing site data may also affect other apps hosted under the same site origin, so review the browser's scope before confirming.

## Delete exported backups

Clearing app data, clearing browser data, or uninstalling does not remove `.ocbackup` files that you exported to Documents, Downloads, cloud storage, email, or another app. Delete each exported copy from its saved location and empty the relevant trash or recycle bin if required.

## Support data

If you previously emailed support and want the developer to delete that correspondence, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com** from the same address and identify the message. Legal or security obligations may require limited records to be retained where applicable.

Because the developer cannot access data stored only on your device, support cannot remotely view or delete that local data for you.
