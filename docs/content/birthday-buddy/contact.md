# Contact Birthday Buddy Support

For Birthday Buddy support, privacy questions, or policy requests, contact:

- **Developer:** Anand Raja
- **Email:** anand.official.in@gmail.com
- **App:** Birthday Buddy
- **Android package:** `com.actionanand.birthdaybuddy.app`

When reporting a problem, include your Android or browser version, the feature involved, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send names, dates, contact data, personal photos, PINs, backup passwords, or `.ocbackup` files unless necessary and you understand the privacy risk.

Birthday Buddy has no developer-operated user account or cloud database. Support cannot remotely inspect or delete information stored only on your device.
