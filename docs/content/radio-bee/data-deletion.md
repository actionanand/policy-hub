# Delete Radio Bee Data

Radio Bee does not require an account. Favorites, home-station choices, interface preferences, filters, and cached catalogues are stored locally.

## Android app

Open **Android Settings → Apps → Radio Bee → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data**, or uninstall Radio Bee. Exact menu labels may vary by device. This removes locally stored app preferences and cache.

## Web app

Open browser settings for **actionanand.github.io** and clear the Radio Bee site's data and storage. Depending on your browser, clearing the shared site origin may affect other sites hosted on that origin.

## Contact form submissions

If you previously submitted the optional Google Forms contact form, clearing app data will not erase that submission. To request access, correction, or deletion, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com** and describe the submission you want reviewed. Requests are handled subject to applicable retention requirements.

Broadcasters, streaming hosts, and hosting providers independently control their logs and data; deleting Radio Bee's local data does not delete those external records.
