# Radio Bee Support

For help with Radio Bee, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com** or use [Contact Us in Radio Bee](https://actionanand.github.io/radio-bee/contact).

When reporting a problem, include the app version, Android version or browser, station name or URL, and a brief description of what happened. Avoid sharing passwords or sensitive information.

## Common questions

- **A station does not play:** Check your internet connection, try another station, and retry later. Broadcasters may change or restrict their stream URLs.
- **A station's information is wrong:** Send the station name, correct website or stream link, and the correction through Contact Us.
- **Favorites or settings disappeared:** These are stored locally and may be removed when device or browser app data is cleared.
- **A station or logo raises a rights concern:** Provide the specific listing or URL and the basis for your request so it can be reviewed.
