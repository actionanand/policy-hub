# Delete Card Nest Data

Card Nest has no online account or developer-operated cloud database. Its app
data is stored on your device.

## Delete individual records

Cards, transactions, categories, loans, EMIs, receipt attachments, and related
records can be deleted from their corresponding app screens. Some deletions can
also update linked balances or related records.

## Delete older transaction history

Open **Settings → Backup & data**, choose a 3, 5, 7, or 10-year retention
period, and select **Clean older data**. Review and confirm the deletion.

## Delete all Card Nest data

Open **Settings → Backup & data → Delete all data**, review the warning, and
confirm. If an application PIN exists, authorize the action using the PIN or an
enabled Android biometric. This removes the Card Nest database and settings
stored by the app.

You can also clear Card Nest storage from Android system settings or uninstall
the app.

## Delete files outside the app

Deleting app data does not remove `.cnbak` backups, CSV files, PDF reports, or
copies shared with another app or storage provider. Delete those files
separately from every destination.

Because the developer does not hold Card Nest account data, emailing support
cannot delete records stored on your device.
