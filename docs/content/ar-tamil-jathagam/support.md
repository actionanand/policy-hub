# AR Tamil Jathagam Support

For support, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com**.

Include the app version, Android or browser version, device model, affected feature, and safe reproduction steps. Do not send passwords, cipher keys, complete birth profiles, Google Sheet contents, or unredacted reports.

## Common help

- **A chart looks incorrect:** verify the birth date, exact time, place, coordinates, time zone, and optional sunrise setting.
- **Online Panchanga does not load:** check internet access and retry later; external services can be unavailable or rate-limited.
- **Cloud profiles do not load:** confirm the configured published Google Sheet is available and that the correct cipher key is set.
- **Forgotten password or cipher key:** these cannot be recovered by the developer. Clearing app storage removes local profiles and preferences.
- **PDF or image export fails:** choose an available save, print, or sharing destination and confirm sufficient storage.
