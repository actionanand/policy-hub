# Delete AR Tamil Jathagam Data

The app has no general-purpose developer-operated user account.

## Delete local profiles and settings

Remove individual profiles from the saved-user interface. To remove every local profile, login session, cipher key, language choice, and preference, clear AR Tamil Jathagam storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. In a browser, clear the site's localStorage, cache, and site data.

## Delete external copies

Clearing the app does not delete rows in a configured Google Sheet, clipboard history, downloaded timeline images, or PDF reports saved, printed, or shared elsewhere. Delete Sheet rows through the relevant Google account or sheet owner, clear clipboard history where supported, and delete files from every destination.

The developer cannot remotely delete data held only on your device or in a third-party account the developer does not control. For help, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com** without attaching sensitive birth information.
