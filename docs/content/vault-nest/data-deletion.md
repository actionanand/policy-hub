# Delete Vault Nest Data

Vault Nest has no online account or developer-operated cloud vault. App data is
stored on your device and can be removed without contacting the developer.

## Delete individual items

Move an item to **Trash**, then permanently delete it from Trash or empty Trash.
Otherwise it is removed automatically after the configured 7, 30, or 90-day
retention period. Selecting **Never** keeps trashed items until you delete them.

## Delete all saved vault items

Open **Settings → Data → Clear vault database** and confirm the warning. This
permanently deletes saved vault items while retaining the master-password setup
and preferences.

## Delete the account and complete local database

Open the Vault Nest navigation menu, select **Remove account**, enter the current
master password, acknowledge the warning, and confirm deletion. This removes
the local vault database, preferences, biometric unlock material, temporary
credential-copy values, and encrypted intrusion evidence.

You can also clear Vault Nest's storage from Android system settings or
uninstall the app.

## Delete files outside the app

Removing app data does not delete `.vaultpack` backups, plaintext CSV exports,
downloaded evidence images, clipboard contents, or copies shared with other
apps or storage providers. Delete those separately from every location where
you saved or shared them.

Because the developer does not hold a cloud vault, emailing support cannot
delete data stored on your device.
