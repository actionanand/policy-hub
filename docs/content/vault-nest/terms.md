## Use of Vault Nest

Vault Nest is provided as a local password and sensitive-information management
tool. You are responsible for the information you store, the strength and
confidentiality of your master password, and lawful use of the app.

Do not use Vault Nest to store or share information you do not have the right to
possess, or to support unlawful access or activity.

## Master Password and Recovery

Vault Nest does not operate an account or password-recovery service. If you lose
your master password, your encrypted vault may become permanently inaccessible.
The developer cannot decrypt or recover it.

Optional four-character easy login is less secure than the full master
password. Optional failed-attempt limits can permanently delete the local vault
when reached. Enable these features only after reviewing their warnings.

## Security Responsibilities

No security system can guarantee complete protection. Keep Android and Vault
Nest updated, secure the device, use a unique master password, review copied or
shared fields carefully, and do not expose secrets in support messages.

You are responsible for reviewing and removing sensitive values from the system
clipboard and from any application used through Android's sharing features.

## Backups and Exports

You are responsible for creating, testing, protecting, and retaining backups.
Keep the backup passphrase and original master password safe; both may be needed
after restoration.

Encrypted `.vaultpack` files remain sensitive. CSV exports are not encrypted
and may contain readable credentials. Securely delete plaintext exports after
their intended use.
