# Expenzo Support

For Expenzo support, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com**.

Include the app version, Android or browser version, device model, affected feature, and safe reproduction steps. Do not send passwords, PINs, access tokens, full financial records, or unredacted exports.

## Common help

- **Data does not load:** check internet access and confirm that the configured Google Sheet and Apps Script remain available.
- **Old data appears:** refresh the app. Expenzo may show its last local cache when a request fails.
- **Forgotten login or PIN:** credentials cannot be recovered by the developer. Clearing app storage also removes local cache and settings.
- **Biometric unlock is unavailable:** use the PIN and confirm Android has a supported enrolled biometric.
- **Export fails:** choose an available save or sharing destination and confirm the destination has enough storage.
