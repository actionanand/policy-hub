# Delete Expenzo Data

Expenzo has no general-purpose developer-operated user account.

## Delete local data

To remove all cached records, login state, preferences, and app-lock settings, clear Expenzo storage in Android system settings or uninstall the app. In a browser, clear the site's localStorage, cache, and site data.

## Delete external data and exports

Clearing Expenzo does not delete source records in configured Google Sheets, Google Apps Script logs, or CSV and PDF files saved or shared outside the app. Delete source records through the relevant Google account or sheet owner and delete exports from every destination.

The developer cannot remotely delete records that exist only on your device or in a Google resource the developer does not control. For assistance, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com** without attaching sensitive financial data.
