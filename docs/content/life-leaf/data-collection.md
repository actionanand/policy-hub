# Life Leaf Data Collection

**Last reviewed:** August 23, 2026

## Data stored locally

Life Leaf can store diary titles and rich-text content, dates and times, moods, tags, favourites, archive or trash status, optional places and weather notes, attachments, reminder choices, and preferences on your device. Optional PIN verification data is stored as a salted PBKDF2-SHA-256 verifier. Android handles biometric verification without giving the app biometric templates.

## Optional local notifications

When you enable a reminder, Life Leaf requests notification permission when required and schedules the selected reminder locally. Schedules can include the chosen time, selected weekdays, and reminder category. Notification wording is generic and does not reveal diary content. The schedule is not sent to the developer.

## User-initiated files and sharing

Backups, restores, attachments, and content shared into Life Leaf are processed only after you choose the relevant file or sharing action. Shared text or an image opens as an unsaved draft. An external destination receives a backup or file only when you choose to save or share it there.

## Data collected by the developer

Life Leaf has no account, developer-operated cloud, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting service, or tracker. The developer does not receive diary data, attachments, reminders, security credentials, or usage telemetry. A support email contains only what you choose to send.

## Google Play Data safety context

App information and optional reminder processing stay on the device and are not transmitted to the developer. Google Play generally defines collection as transmission off the device. The Play Console declaration must still be reviewed for each released app version and for user-initiated transfers to external destinations.
