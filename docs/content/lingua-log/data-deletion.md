# Delete Lingua Log Data

Lingua Log has no general-purpose developer-operated user account.

## Delete local records

Logging out removes the local login session. To remove the offline Sheet cache, theme, login, PIN, and biometric settings together, clear Lingua Log storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. In a browser, clear the site's localStorage, cache, and site data.

## Delete Google Sheet and clipboard records

Lingua Log does not directly delete Google Sheet rows. Remove them through the relevant Google account or ask the Sheet owner. Clearing app data does not delete Sheet rows, clipboard history, pasted documents, or copies held by other apps. Delete each external copy separately.

The developer cannot remotely delete content that exists only on your device or in a third-party account the developer does not control. For help, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com** without attaching confidential entries.
