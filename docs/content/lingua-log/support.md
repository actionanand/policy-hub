# Lingua Log Support

For support, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com**.

Include the app version, Android or browser version, device model, affected feature, and safe reproduction steps. Do not send passwords, PINs, private Sheet links, confidential language entries, or unredacted clipboard data.

## Common help

- **Entries do not load:** check internet access and confirm the configured Google Sheet and tab are published or shared appropriately.
- **Offline data is old:** reconnect and reload. Android retains only the latest cached entries.
- **Protected entries are hidden:** log in locally. The Protected flag does not change Google Sheet permissions.
- **A copied row pastes incorrectly:** copy the header and row again, then paste into Google Sheets without altering tab separators.
- **Forgotten login or PIN:** credentials cannot be recovered by the developer. Clearing app storage also removes local cache and settings.
- **Biometric unlock is unavailable:** use the PIN and confirm Android has a supported enrolled biometric.
