# AR Blogz Support

For questions, corrections, privacy requests, or accessibility issues, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com** or use the website contact form.

Include the article URL, a concise description, and reliable sources when suggesting a correction. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, private medical records, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## Common help

- **A page is missing:** try the other deployment link or refresh after clearing a stale cache.
- **An embed does not load:** privacy controls, network filtering, regional restrictions, or the external provider may block it. Open the cited source directly.
- **Language or theme is wrong:** choose the preferred option again or clear the site's localStorage.
- **Protected content will not unlock:** confirm the password and browser storage access. The publisher cannot recover a password entered only in your browser.
- **A contact form submission needs deletion:** email the publisher with the approximate submission date, email address used, and inquiry purpose.
- **Medical or urgent question:** contact a qualified professional or local emergency service; blog support cannot provide clinical advice.
