# Delete AR Blogz Data

AR Blogz has no reader account.

## Delete browser data

Clear AR Blogz cookies, localStorage, cache, and site permissions in your browser to remove theme, language, and password-access records. This does not delete information held by hosting providers or external services.

## Delete a contact submission

Email **anand.official.in@gmail.com** and identify the Google Form submission using the approximate date, email address used, and inquiry purpose. Do not repeat the full message or include additional sensitive information. The publisher will handle a valid request subject to identity verification, applicable law, and legitimate retention needs.

## Third-party data

Requests to delete hosting logs, embedded-service activity, payment records, social-platform information, or data held by an external link must be directed to the relevant provider. AR Blogz cannot delete records controlled solely by a third party.
