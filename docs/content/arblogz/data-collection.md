# AR Blogz Data Collection

**Last reviewed:** August 1, 2026

## Information submitted

The contact form sends your name, email, optional phone number, inquiry purpose, and message to Google Forms. Google and the form owner process and retain the submission.

## Local information

The browser stores theme and language choices. A password-protected article can store a password-hash access record and timestamp for up to 24 hours. Site search runs locally.

## Technical and third-party information

Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages receives ordinary hosting logs. Daily-update sources, embeds, linked services, and donation providers receive requests when used. Analytics and comments are supported by the code but are currently disabled.
