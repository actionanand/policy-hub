# Delete LocalTell Data

LocalTell has no online account or developer-operated cloud database. Its data is stored on your Android device.

## Remove an offline pack

Open **Offline data**, select an installed pack, and choose **Remove**. This removes that local pack from LocalTell.

## Clear Journey history

Open **Journey** and choose **Clear** to remove the locally stored Journey entries.

## Delete all local data

Clear LocalTell storage in Android system settings or uninstall the app. This removes LocalTell's app-private offline packs, preferences, and Journey history. Android backup and device transfer do not retain this app data.

Because the developer does not receive or store this data, emailing support cannot delete records from your device.
