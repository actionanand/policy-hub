shortDescription: Offline approximate-area lookup from your serving cellular network.
---

LocalTell helps you identify a locality from your serving cellular network and, when needed, a short on-device GNSS fix.

Offline by design

• Download only the state or area packs you need
• Match serving cell information against a local offline database
• Use results without sending your cell data to a developer service
• Refresh available packs and install updates when you choose
• Share a location with Easy codes or a Google Maps link when you choose

Approximate-area results

• See the best available approximate area, district, and state match
• Review cellular radio technology, network information, and confidence
• Locality names are resolved from offline packs; GNSS coordinates and Easy codes reflect the precision of the original device fix

Journey mode

• Optionally record an on-device history when the resolved area changes
• Run while you choose to start Journey mode with an Android foreground-service notification
• Clear Journey history at any time

Privacy

LocalTell has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or developer cloud. Android location permission enables cellular lookup and a short on-device GNSS fix when needed. LocalTell does not continuously track or upload your location.

Cellular estimates may be unavailable or inaccurate. Do not use LocalTell for emergency response, navigation, safety-critical decisions, or exact location.
