Qurio brings multilingual learning, interactive practice, progress tracking, and structured exam preparation into one clear app.

Learn by Subject

• Browse class-wise subjects and study material
• Read focused notes with diagrams and explanations
• Choose English, Tamil, or Hindi where translations are available
• Continue from recent learning activity

Practice with Quizzes

• Take timed and untimed quizzes
• Use hints and review answer explanations
• See correct, wrong, and unanswered counts
• Review scores and practice history
• Revisit topics that need attention

Prepare for Exams

• Follow daily and weekly study plans
• Review phases, syllabus, strategy, and progress
• Track topic, revision, and timetable tasks
• Organize preparation around target dates

Progress Across Devices

• Sign in with a verified account
• Synchronize lessons, attempts, preferences, and exam-plan progress
• Keep recent progress cached locally
• Reset your password securely by email

Practice Reminders

• Choose days and time for Android practice reminders
• Receive local notifications without promotional tracking
• Disable reminders whenever you want

Account and Device Security

• Email verification and account approval protect access
• Optional PIN lock works on web and Android
• Optional biometric unlock works on supported Android devices
• Role-based owner and administrator controls protect accounts

Qurio is responsive on phones, tablets, and browsers and supports light, dark, and system appearance settings.

Qurio has no advertisements and no developer-operated analytics or tracking SDK. Internet access is required for authentication, synchronization, and current learning content.
