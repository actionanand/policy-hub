Flowra provides private period and cycle record keeping, descriptive statistics,
and uncertain on-device estimates. It is not a medical device or medical
service and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, contraception, fertility
advice, or emergency guidance.

Do not rely on Flowra predictions to prevent or achieve pregnancy or to make a
medical decision. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional for
severe pain, unusually heavy bleeding, pregnancy concerns, persistent or sudden
changes, or any symptom that concerns you. Contact local emergency services for
an emergency.

## Profiles and records

You are responsible for the accuracy of information you enter and for obtaining
appropriate permission before creating a profile for another person. A parent
or guardian should supervise use involving a child. Keep each person's profile
separate and enter only information reasonably needed for tracking.

Predictions may be inaccurate, especially with limited history, irregular
cycles, pregnancy, postpartum changes, perimenopause, illness, medication, or
other life changes.

protections depend on Android and device capabilities and cannot guarantee that
information will never be exposed.

forgotten password. You are responsible for safeguarding, transferring,
verifying, and deleting all exported or backup files.

Restoring a backup replaces local Flowra records. Review the selected file and
keep a current backup before restoring important data.
