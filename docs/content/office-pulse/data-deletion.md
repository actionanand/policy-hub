# Delete Office Pulse Data

Office Pulse has no general-purpose developer-operated user account.

## Delete local records

Delete individual tasks, memos, cue cards, reminders, or other editable records
from their corresponding screens where a delete action is available. Logging
out removes the local successful-login record.

To remove all local records, clear Office Pulse storage in Android system
settings or uninstall the app. In a browser, clear the site's localStorage,
cache, and site data.

## Delete external records and files

Clearing app data does not delete:

- Information already submitted to a Google Form
- Rows or source data stored in configured Google Sheets
- PDF reports saved or shared outside Office Pulse

Delete PDF copies from every destination. Contact the relevant form or sheet
owner or workplace administrator to request deletion of externally held data,
subject to their retention requirements.

Because the developer does not hold a general Office Pulse cloud account,
emailing support cannot delete records that exist only on your device or in a
third party's Google Workspace.
