# Office Pulse Support

For Office Pulse support, email **anand.official.in@gmail.com**.

Include the app version, Android or browser version, device model, affected
feature, and safe reproduction steps. Do not email workplace secrets, Google
credentials, Jira tokens, passwords, PINs, full payment-card details, or
unredacted reports.

## Common help

- **Sheet data is missing:** confirm internet access and that the configured
  Google Sheet and tab are published or shared appropriately.
- **A form will not submit:** review the prefilled fields, network access, and
  the configured Google Form's availability.
- **Forgotten local login or PIN:** these credentials are not recoverable by the
  developer. Clearing app storage also removes local records and settings.
- **Biometric unlock unavailable:** use the PIN and confirm Android has a
  supported enrolled biometric.
- **Reminder missing:** grant notification access and review exact-alarm,
  battery, lock-screen, and system-time settings.
- **PDF export fails:** choose an available save or sharing destination, or
  allow browser popups and use the print dialog.
